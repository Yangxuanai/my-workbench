A Better Trader
Every day is a new opportunity. I review my trades, not with regret, but with curiosity. I look for patterns in my mistakes and patterns in my wins. The market does not care about my feelings, so I must master them. A good trader is not one who never loses, but one who loses small and lets winners run. Discipline is built one day at a time, one trade at a time.
每天都是新机会。我复盘交易，不是为了后悔，而是出于好奇。我在错误和盈利中寻找规律。市场不在乎我的情绪，所以我必须掌控它。好的交易者不是从不亏损，而是亏小赚大。纪律是日复一日、一笔一笔建立起来的。
